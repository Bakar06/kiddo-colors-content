Kiddo Colors – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026 · Publisher: SBM Labs

Kiddo Colors is a coloring app for the whole family. We built it to be safe and simple to use, and we collect no personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party advertising or analytics tools. We do not collect names, e-mail addresses, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data from children or parents.

Drawings and settings

Pictures that your child colors, along with app settings such as the chosen language, are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it.

Downloading new coloring pages

About once a day the app checks a public list of new coloring pages and downloads any new pictures from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). Like any internet request, GitHub can technically see the device's IP address when the app asks for these files. We do not receive or store this information and no data about your child is sent.

Purchases

The optional "Premium" unlock is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Payment details are processed by Apple and are never visible to us. A parental check is shown before any purchase screen opens.

Family use

The app is suitable for all ages, including children, and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone. It contains no ads and no social features.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Kiddo Colors ist eine Mal-App für die ganze Familie. Sie wurde so entwickelt, dass sie sicher und einfach zu bedienen ist, und wir erheben keinerlei personenbezogene Daten.

Nichts. Die App hat keine Konten, keine Registrierung, keine Werbung, keine Analyse, kein Tracking und keine Werbe- oder Analysewerkzeuge von Drittanbietern. Wir erheben weder Namen, E-Mail-Adressen, Standort, Kontakte, Fotos noch sonstige personenbezogene Daten von Kindern oder Eltern.
